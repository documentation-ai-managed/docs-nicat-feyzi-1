## Business Setup and Launch Assistance

You start your e-commerce journey with our end-to-end setup service. We configure your Shopify store, optimize Amazon product listings, and prepare Kickstarter pages for maximum impact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Platform" icon="shopping-bag">
    Select Shopify, Amazon, or Kickstarter based on your product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store Configuration">
    We set up themes, payments, and shipping.
    
````liquid
<!-- Sample Shopify theme snippet -->
<div class="product-card">
  {% for product in collections.frontpage.products %}
    <a href="{{ product.url }}">
      <img src="{{ product.featured_image | img_url: '300x300' }}" alt="{{ product.title }}">
      <h3>{{ product.title }}</h3>
      <span>{{ product.price | money }}</span>
    </a>
  {% endfor %}
</div>
````
  </Step>

    Test and launch with our quality checks.
  </Step>
