## Overview

Follow these steps to set up your Amazon store with MakeItEarn support.

<Steps>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your business info">
    ````
    Enter your business details and verify identity.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="List first product">
    ````
    Use MakeItEarn tips to optimize your product listing and images.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
