---
title: Cannot Connect Shopify Store
description: Quick steps to fix Shopify connection issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Shopify store fails to link
- Error: "Authorization failed"

## Causes

- Wrong shop URL
- Missing permissions

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check store URL">
    ````
    Ensure your Shopify URL is correct. Use only yourshop.myshopify.com format.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify app permissions">
    ````
    Grant required permissions to MakeItEarn during the connection process.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
