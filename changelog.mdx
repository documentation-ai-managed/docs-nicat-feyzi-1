## How to Get Notified

Follow these steps to ensure you never miss an update:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Notifications" icon="bell">
    Log in to your dashboard at `https://dashboard.example.com`.
    
    Navigate to **Account Settings** > **Notifications**.
    
    Toggle on **Changelog Updates** and **New Training Alerts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join Community" icon="users">
    Visit the community section and subscribe to update channels.
    
    Enable email digests for weekly summaries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Integrations" icon="zap">
    Verify your platform webhooks at `https://api.example.com/webhooks`.
    
    Test with this sample payload:
    
````json
{
  "event": "update",
  "version": "v2.1.0",
  "changes": ["new-training"]
}
````
    
  </Step>
</Steps>
