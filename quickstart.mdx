## Prerequisites

Before diving in, ensure you have the following ready:

<Callout kind="info">

- A valid email address for account verification
- Basic business details: company name, niche (e.g., fashion, electronics), and target market
- Preferred e-commerce platform: Shopify, Amazon, or Kickstarter
- Access to a modern web browser like Chrome or Firefox

</Callout>

These prepare you for a smooth onboarding process with MakeItEarn's expert guidance.

## Create Your Account

Sign up takes under 2 minutes. Follow these steps to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit MakeItEarn" icon="globe">
    Navigate to [https://app.makeitearn.com/signup](https://app.makeitearn.com/signup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, create a strong password, and submit the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link from `no-reply@makeitearn.com` and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the login page and sign in with your credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You'll land on your personalized dashboard.

## Book Your Initial Consultation

Schedule a free 30-minute call with a MakeItEarn e-commerce specialist.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Scheduler" icon="calendar">
    From your dashboard, click **Book Consultation** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Slot" icon="clock">
    Choose a date and time that fits your schedule. Consultations are available Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM EST.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Details" icon="check-circle">
    Fill in your business niche and goals, then submit. You'll receive a Zoom link via email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Prepare questions about your niche and platform choice to maximize the session.
</Callout>

## Complete Business Setup Checklist

Use this checklist during or after your consultation to profile your business.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Core Profile" icon="settings">
    - Business name and legal entity
    - Target audience demographics
    - Product sourcing strategy
  </Card>
  <Card title="Financial Basics" icon="dollar-sign">
    - Startup budget range
    - Projected monthly revenue goals
    - Payment processor preferences (e.g., Stripe, PayPal)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Marketing Plan" icon="trending-up">
    - Social media channels
    - Email list size
    - Initial ad spend allocation
  </Card>
  <Card title="Operations" icon="package">
    - Inventory management needs
    - Shipping provider options
    - Customer support tools
  </Card>
</Columns>

Update your profile in the dashboard under **Business Settings**.

## Select and Set Up Your Platform

Choose your primary platform and begin setup. MakeItEarn provides tailored support for each.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Shopify" icon="shopping-cart">
    Ideal for custom stores with full control.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create Store">
        Visit [shopify.com](https://shopify.com) and start a free trial.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install CLI" icon="terminal">
````bash
npm install -g @shopify/cli @shopify/theme
````
      </Step>
      <Step title="Theme Setup">
        Run `shopify theme init` in your project directory.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Amazon" icon="shopping-bag">
    Perfect for high-volume sales via marketplace.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Seller Central">
        Sign up at [sellercentral.amazon.com](https://sellercentral.amazon.com).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify Account">
        Upload business docs and bank details.
      </Step>
      <Step title="List Products">
        Use the bulk upload template for initial inventory.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Kickstarter" icon="gift">
    Great for crowdfunding product launches.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create Account">
        Go to [kickstarter.com](https://kickstarter.com) and sign up.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Project Setup">
        Draft your campaign with video, story, and rewards.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Launch Prep">
        Submit for review 7 days in advance.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
